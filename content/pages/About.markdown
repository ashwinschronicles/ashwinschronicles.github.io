# Hello, I'm Ashwin!

I am a **Research Software Engineer (RSE) and Physicist** currently based in Germany, working at the intersection of quantum computing, high-performance computing, and scientific instrumentation.

---

## What I Do

By day, I work at the **Jülich Supercomputing Centre (JSC)**, Forschungszentrum Jülich. My main focus is integrating **quantum computers with classical High-Performance Computing (HPC)** systems—building the infrastructure for hybrid quantum-classical computing, interfacing quantum processing units (QPUs), and benchmarking scientific workloads on them.

Before joining JSC, I worked as an RSE at the **Max Planck Institute for the Structure and Dynamics of Matter (MPSD)** in Hamburg, developing research tools for computational physics.

My academic foundation comes from **BITS Pilani, K.K. Birla Goa Campus** (Batch of 2017–2022), where I completed a dual degree:

- **B.E. in Electronics & Instrumentation**
- **Integrated M.Sc. in Physics**

This blend of physics theory and practical hardware/software engineering has shaped how I approach problems: I enjoy understanding the low-level physics of a system just as much as writing clean, scalable software to control or simulate it.

---

## Beyond the Terminal

When I step away from code, compute clusters, and quantum computers, a few interests keep me grounded:

- **Flute & Acoustic Experiments:** Music is a big part of my life. I play the Indian classical flute (Bansuri). Over time, my physics curiosity spilled over into instrument-making: collaborating on designing and 3D-printing custom flutes, calculating acoustic resonance formulas, experimenting with conical bores, and testing how subtle design changes shape tone and timbre.
- **Forró Dancing:** I fell in love with Forró—a partner dance from northeastern Brazil. You can frequently find me on the social dance floor in Aachen, Cologne, or Düsseldorf, learning leader/follower dynamics, musicality, and connecting with a wonderful cross-cultural dance community.
- **Running & Parkrun:** I run to clear my mind. Saturday morning 5K Parkruns are a weekend staple, rain, frost, or shine, along with longer trail and neighborhood runs.
- **Cycling:** Cycling is both my daily commute and weekend escape through the Rhineland. I also enjoy the mechanical side of things—tuning derailleurs, fixing chains, and maintaining bikes myself.
- **Electronics & Hardware Tinkering:** My workbench is rarely clear. I enjoy tinkering with physical hardware: setting up small DIY solar panel rigs, designing battery management circuits, hacking microcontrollers (ESP8266/ESP32, Arduino) for IoT controls, and soldering together sensor prototypes.

## Why This Blog?

I started **Ashwin's Chronicles** as a personal, open-access laboratory notebook.

Research and engineering can often feel fragmented. Writing down how I designed a cryogenic probe, solved an obscure Linux/HPC issue, simulated a Josephson junction, or built an IoT device helps me consolidate my understanding so I do not forget. At the same time, if an article saves hours for another student, engineer, or curious tinkerer facing the same problem, that is the greatest reward.
